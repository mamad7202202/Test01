Overview

Dragon Agent Mobile is the pocket version of the desktop dragon-agent: an AI chat agent for Android and iOS that remembers you. Most mobile AI chat apps forget everything when you close them. This one keeps facts, standing rules and past sessions on your device, so a new chat picks up where you left off.

You bring your own API key, choose a provider and a model, and start talking. Keys, memories and transcripts stay on your device.

The source is MIT-licensed on GitHub. Found a problem? Open an issue.
